# Research Librarian AI v10.8.0 — Dataset Discovery & Data Fitness Intelligence

v10.8.0 turns accepted research gaps and original-research opportunities into governed data-planning work. It adds durable data requirements, dataset candidate metadata, variable registries, question-specific human fitness assessments, structural coverage matrices, reproducible snapshots, non-executing computational handoffs to Workspace/Lab/Workbench/Catalyst Data, and candidate handoffs to Platform Core.

The engine does not certify dataset quality, automatically select a best dataset, infer variable meaning, ingest data, or make scientific-validity claims.
