# Research Librarian AI v10.7.0 — Research Gap & Novelty Intelligence

v10.7.0 adds a durable research-gap and novelty-planning layer above v10.5 Scholarly Citation & Literature Intelligence and v10.6 Argument, Claim & Counterclaim Intelligence.

## Added

- Durable research gap/novelty projects with optional v10.6 argument-intelligence and v10.5 literature-intelligence lineage.
- Descriptive structural gap signals from explicitly recorded unresolved tensions, claims without recorded evidence links, human-declared literature gaps, and sparse human-labeled literature strands.
- Human-recorded research-gap candidates across evidence, methodology, population, context, geography, time, data, measurement, replication, contradiction, theory, implementation, and other scoped categories.
- Explicit human acceptance/rejection of gap candidates; structural signals are never automatically promoted into validated gaps.
- Novelty candidates across research questions, methods, datasets, populations, contexts, synthesis, mechanisms, replication, applications, and other scoped categories.
- Mandatory comparison basis for novelty candidates plus explicit human disposition.
- Original-research opportunity records connecting accepted gaps, novelty candidates, method references, data requirements, feasibility notes, and limitations.
- Descriptive gap/novelty landscape with no priority score or automated novelty score.
- Non-writing handoff back into research-question/design planning.
- Governed Platform Core candidates without automatic promotion.
- Durable `research-gap-novelty-intelligence-snapshot` jobs and immutable snapshots.
- Unified v10 environment binding as `research-gap-novelty-plan`.
- Postgres migration `022_research_gap_novelty_intelligence.sql`.

## Governance

A structural signal is not a validated research gap. An accepted novelty candidate is not proof of originality. Absence of registered evidence is not evidence of absence. Research opportunities are planning candidates, not originality certifications. Automatic gap certification, novelty certification, originality claims, priority ranking, and truth promotion remain disabled. Platform Core remains the authority for governed research objects.
