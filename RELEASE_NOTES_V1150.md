# Research Librarian AI v11.5.0 — Reproduction & Replication Intelligence

v11.5.0 adds a durable, governed layer for planning, executing through specialist runtimes, recording, and reviewing reproduction and replication work.

The release explicitly distinguishes computational/analytical reproduction from independent replication. It binds preregistered study lineage and can inherit v11.2 statistical plans, v11.3 causal designs, and v11.4 simulation/model studies. It adds reproduction attempts, replication studies, comparability criteria, environment manifests, disclosed deviations, execution receipts, human outcome assessments, comparison matrices, lineage maps, non-executing runtime handoffs, Platform Core candidates, immutable snapshots, authenticated APIs, durable jobs, unified-environment bindings, and Postgres migration 030.

Governance is human-centered. The engine does not automatically declare a result reproduced or replicated, accept or reject a claim, infer causality, execute research, or promote truth. An output match is not treated as independent replication evidence, and a differing replication result is not automatically treated as falsification.
