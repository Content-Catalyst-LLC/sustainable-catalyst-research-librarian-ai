# Research Librarian AI v10.2.0 — Research Design & Methodology Planner

v10.2.0 turns the structured questions and candidate hypotheses introduced in v10.1 into reproducible research-design plans. The planner can surface multiple design families appropriate to the declared question type, preserve their assumptions and validity threats, identify data/sampling needs, record analysis candidates and execution targets, and prepare governed handoffs for Platform Core and specialist runtimes.

The release intentionally does **not** make a scientific method choice for the researcher. Method comparison is descriptive, human preference/review is explicit, causal identification is not inferred, and execution remains with Workspace, Research Lab, Analytics R, Workbench, or another appropriate runtime.
