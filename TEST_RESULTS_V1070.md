- Structural signals are observations, not validated research gaps.

- Human acceptance is required for novelty candidates.
- Novelty candidates require an explicit comparison basis.
- Accepted novelty candidates do not certify originality.
- Research opportunities are planning candidates, not originality certifications.
- Absence of registered evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Automatic gap certification is disabled.
- Automatic novelty certification is disabled.
- Automatic originality claims are disabled.
- Automatic priority ranking is disabled.
- Automatic truth promotion is disabled.
- Platform Core remains governed research-object authority.
