# Research Librarian AI v10.4.0 — Systematic Review & Evidence Synthesis Intelligence

v10.4.0 converts the reproducible search protocols introduced in v10.3 into durable systematic-review workflows. Reviews can register retrieved candidate studies, preserve reviewer-attributed title/abstract and full-text screening decisions, build structured extraction matrices, record risk-of-bias and evidence-certainty judgments, and define narrative or computational synthesis plans.

The release preserves product boundaries: Knowledge Library owns source ingestion/retrieval and document intelligence; Research Librarian owns review orchestration and scholarly workflow records; specialist runtimes execute statistical synthesis; Platform Core remains the governed authority for promoted research/evidence objects.

No study is automatically accepted or excluded. Risk-of-bias judgments and evidence-certainty grades are reviewer records, not machine certifications. Meta-analysis plans are handoffs only until explicitly executed by a specialist runtime.
