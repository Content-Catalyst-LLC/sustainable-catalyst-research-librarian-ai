# Research Librarian AI v9.4.0 — Scholarly Publication, Citation & Research Dissemination Environment

v9.4.0 extends the v9.2 original-research and v9.3 peer-review environments into a durable scholarly dissemination layer.

It adds versioned publication records, human-declared authors and contributors, structured references, citation exports, explicit publication-state transitions, externally assigned identifier records, canonical publication URLs, publication visualization lineage, explicit Knowledge Library handoffs, immutable event history, and frozen publication packages.

## Governance boundaries

The Librarian does not mint DOIs, infer authorship, issue editorial acceptance, publish automatically, certify peer review, or determine truth. External identifiers are recorded as declared identifiers. Accepted and published states require an explicit human decision reference; published state also requires a canonical URL. Knowledge Library handoffs remain explicit import-ready packages rather than automatic publication actions. Platform Core publication promotion remains an explicit follow-on action.
