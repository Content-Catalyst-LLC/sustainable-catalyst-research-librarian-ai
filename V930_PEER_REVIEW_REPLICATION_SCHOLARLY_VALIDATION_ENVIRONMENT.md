# Research Librarian AI v9.3.0 — Peer Review, Replication & Scholarly Validation Environment

v9.3.0 extends the original-research study environment with durable human peer-review and replication lineage.

## Review cycle
- create explicit review rounds bound to a v9.2 study
- assign reviewers and record declared conflicts
- preserve structured human reviewer reports and recommendations
- preserve author responses and revision submissions
- register independent replication/reanalysis/robustness attempts with runtime, artifact, result, source, and evidence references
- record human editorial decisions and superseding decisions
- freeze a review/replication package with an immutable content hash

## Governance
Readiness means workflow completeness, not scientific validity. Reviewer recommendations, replication outcomes, and editorial decisions are reported as human-authored records. Research Librarian does not infer acceptance, rejection, replication success, causal validity, scientific truth, or publication worthiness.

## Relationship to v9.2
The v9.2 scholarly study remains the authoritative protocol/result/manuscript object. v9.3 stores review-cycle state separately and links it by study ID, study fingerprint, protocol hash, result IDs, evidence references, and revision references.
