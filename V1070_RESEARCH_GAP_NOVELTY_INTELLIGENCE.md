# Research Librarian AI v10.7.0 — Research Gap & Novelty Intelligence

This release adds durable research-gap and novelty planning above v10.5 literature intelligence and v10.6 argument intelligence.

## Capabilities

- Descriptive structural gap signals from explicitly recorded argument/literature state.
- Human-recorded gap candidates across evidence, methods, population, context, geography, time, data, measurement, replication, contradiction, theory, and implementation.
- Explicit human acceptance/rejection of scoped research gaps.
- Novelty candidates with mandatory comparison basis and explicit human disposition.
- Original-research opportunity records linked to gaps and novelty candidates.
- Descriptive gap/novelty landscape; no priority or novelty score.
- Non-writing research-planning and Platform Core candidate handoffs.
- Immutable snapshots and unified research-environment bindings.

## Governance

A structural signal is not a validated research gap. An accepted novelty candidate is not proof of originality. Absence of registered evidence is not evidence of absence. Research opportunities are planning candidates. Platform Core remains the governed research-object authority and human scholarly judgment is preserved.
