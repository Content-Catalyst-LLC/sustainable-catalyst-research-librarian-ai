# Research Librarian AI v11.4.0 — Simulation & Model Study Planner

v11.4.0 adds a durable prospective planning layer for simulations and model studies. It inherits v11.3 causal-design lineage and introduces explicit model specifications, variables, parameters, scenario sets, stochastic assumptions, calibration/validation plans, uncertainty and sensitivity plans, ensemble plans, compute budgets, stopping criteria, output commitments, human approvals, non-executing runtime handoffs, Platform Core candidates, snapshots, authenticated APIs, unified-environment bindings, and Postgres migration 029.

Research Librarian remains the planning/orchestration layer. It does not automatically select models, calibrate or validate models, execute simulations, accept forecasts, infer causality, or promote modeled outputs as truth. Research Lab, Workspace, Workbench, language runtimes, and other specialist compute systems remain responsible for execution.
