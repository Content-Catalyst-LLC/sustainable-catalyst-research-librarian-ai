# Research Librarian AI v9.2.0 — Original Research & Scholarly Research Environment

v9.2.0 turns the v9.1 durable research workflow into a durable scholarly study environment for original research.

## Study lifecycle

1. Create a study linked to a Platform Core project and optionally a v9.1 workflow.
2. Declare the research question, study type, authorship/disclosures, and protocol.
3. Freeze the protocol with a reviewer identity and immutable protocol hash.
4. Record every post-freeze design, data, sampling, measurement, or analysis change as a deviation.
5. Register research results with specialist-runtime, artifact, evidence, statistical, and visual lineage.
6. Add human-authored interpretations with explicit uncertainty/qualification.
7. Add human-authored manuscript sections and citation references.
8. Evaluate publication readiness; unresolved requirements remain explicit blockers.
9. Freeze a content-addressed scholarly reproducibility package with immutable study lineage.

## Authority boundaries

The Research Librarian organizes and preserves study state. Platform Core remains authoritative for governed claims, findings, arguments, evidence, statistical reasoning, visual reasoning, project state, and reproducibility objects. Specialist runtimes compute analytical results. Researchers remain responsible for authorship, interpretation, conclusions, disclosures, and scholarly judgment.

v9.2.0 does not certify peer review, infer truth, accept claims automatically, infer causality, generate authorship, or interpret statistical significance automatically.
