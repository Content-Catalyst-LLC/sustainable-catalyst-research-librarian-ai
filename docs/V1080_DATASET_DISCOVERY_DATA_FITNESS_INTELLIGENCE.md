# Research Librarian AI v10.8.0 — Dataset Discovery & Data Fitness Intelligence

v10.8.0 connects v10.7 research gaps and original-research opportunities to candidate data resources without treating discovery metadata as scientific validation.

## Capabilities
- durable dataset-fitness projects linked to research gaps/opportunities
- human-authored data requirements
- dataset candidate registry with provider, identifiers, provenance, geography, population, time coverage, access and license notes
- explicit variable registry with analytical roles, units, missingness and transformation notes
- structural requirement-to-dataset coverage matrices
- human fitness decisions: pending, fit, fit-with-limitations, not-fit
- computational planning handoffs to Workspace, Research Lab, Workbench and Catalyst Data without automatic execution or ingestion
- Platform Core candidate handoffs without automatic promotion
- immutable reproducibility snapshots and durable async snapshot jobs
- unified scholarly/AI research environment binding as `dataset-fitness-plan`

## Governance
A dataset being discovered does not mean it is fit for a research question. Recorded variable names do not certify semantics. Missing metadata does not prove missing data. Licensing/access notes require human verification. Fitness decisions are explicitly question-specific and human-recorded. No automatic dataset suitability, quality certification, ingestion, scientific-validity certification, or truth promotion occurs.
