# Research Librarian AI v11.1.0 — Study Protocol & Preregistration Engine

## Purpose
Turns Research Program Intelligence workstreams into explicit, auditable study protocols suitable for preregistration workflows.

## Capabilities
- Bind protocols to a v11.0 research program and optional workstream.
- Register hypotheses, outcomes, variables, sampling plans, and analysis commitments.
- Require human approval of analysis commitments before structural preregistration readiness.
- Freeze an immutable preregistration baseline snapshot.
- Record registration target/identifier/URL without claiming an external registry accepted the record.
- Preserve later changes as append-only amendments linked to the original baseline hash.
- Record protocol deviations without automatically judging validity.
- Produce non-executing computational handoffs and Platform Core study-protocol candidates.
- Persist immutable snapshots and durable snapshot jobs.
- Bind study protocols into the unified scholarly/AI research environment.

## Governance
Preregistration is a timestamped methodological commitment, not proof of validity, truth, methodological quality, or successful external registration. Human review remains required. The Librarian does not execute analyses, choose methods, accept hypotheses, certify deviations, or promote truth claims.

## Persistence
Migration 026 creates study protocol, event, and snapshot tables.
