# Research Librarian AI v10.6.0 — Argument, Claim & Counterclaim Intelligence

## Purpose

v10.6.0 converts the structured scholarly landscape from v10.5 into an explicit, reproducible argument-analysis layer. It is designed to support research argumentation without collapsing disagreement into an automated conclusion.

## Durable objects

- argument-intelligence projects
- researcher-recorded claims, subclaims, counterclaims, rebuttals and qualifications
- claim↔evidence relations with source locators and rationale
- claim↔claim relations such as support, challenge, rebuttal, qualification, dependency and alternatives
- assumptions attached to individual claims or the broader argument
- open/partially resolved/resolved tension records
- analysis-scope claim decisions
- immutable argument-intelligence snapshots

## Analytical views

- claim-evidence matrix
- argument map
- contradiction/tension register
- non-writing v8.10 argument-synthesis handoff
- Platform Core governed candidate package

## Lineage

A v10.6 project can bind to a v10.5 literature-intelligence project. Bound scholarly work IDs are validated against that literature project. The literature fingerprint and systematic-review lineage are preserved in the v10.6 record.

## Governance boundaries

- Claim recording does not establish truth.
- `accepted_for_analysis` means only that a reviewer chose to include a claim in the analysis workspace.
- Counterclaims are not generated or rejected automatically.
- Evidence relations are explicit researcher/reviewer records, not inferred evidentiary judgments.
- Contradictions and tensions are preserved rather than automatically resolved.
- No argument ranking, best-argument selection, claim acceptance, evidence-strength scoring or truth promotion occurs automatically.
- Platform Core remains the authority for governed argument/research objects.
- The existing v8.10 argument-synthesis layer remains the promotion/argument-graph contract; v10.6 produces a reviewed handoff rather than replacing it.
