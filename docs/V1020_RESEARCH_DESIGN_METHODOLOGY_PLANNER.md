# Research Librarian AI v10.2.0 — Research Design & Methodology Planner

## Purpose
Convert an approved or developing research-question plan into a reproducible set of **candidate** research designs without automatically selecting a scientific method.

## Added
- Durable methodology-plan records bound to v10.1 question/hypothesis plans.
- Deterministic candidate design scaffolding by question type.
- Sampling, data, assumption, validity-threat, limitation, analysis-candidate, and execution-target fields.
- Side-by-side comparison without automatic ranking.
- Explicit human method preference and review state.
- Governed Platform Core research-design candidate handoff.
- Workspace / Research Lab / Analytics R execution-handoff packets without automatic delivery or execution.
- Immutable methodology snapshots and durable snapshot jobs.
- v10.0 unified-environment binding as `research-design-plan`.

## Governance
The planner produces research-design options and structured planning records. It does not establish scientific validity, causal identification, statistical appropriateness, or truth. Human method selection is required. Platform Core remains the governed research-object authority and specialist runtimes retain execution.
