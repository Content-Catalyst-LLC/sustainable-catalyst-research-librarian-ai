# Research Librarian AI v10.0.0 — Unified Scholarly & AI Research Intelligence Environment

v10.0.0 is the convergence milestone for the Research Librarian scholarly and AI research stack.

A unified environment binds, without copying authority from, the durable research workflow, original scholarly study, peer-review/replication record, publication, research knowledge graph, AI research contexts, RAG evaluations, AI experiments, model-aware lineage, cross-product exchanges, and governed Platform Core object references.

## Core behaviors

- Persistent environment registry with deterministic identity.
- Appendable, de-duplicated component bindings.
- Cross-layer lineage assembly with unresolved-component reporting.
- Structural readiness dimensions for scholarly + AI lineage.
- Unified research dossier spanning question through dissemination/exchange.
- Immutable, content-hashed environment snapshots.
- Durable `unified-research-environment-snapshot` job.

## Governance

The environment is an orchestration and lineage surface, not a new authority. Component authority remains with the source subsystem. Platform Core continues to own governed research/AI objects. Specialist runtimes continue to own computation. Human authors, reviewers, editors, researchers and operators retain scholarly and model-selection judgment.

Structural readiness is not scientific validity. A dossier is an assembled view, not a new source of truth.
