# Research Librarian AI v9.9.0 — Model-Aware Research Intelligence & Cross-Product Exchange

v9.9.0 binds AI-assisted research outputs to governed AI and research-object lineage, then packages selected lineage for explicit cross-product exchange.

## Model-aware lineage

A durable record may reference Platform Core model/model-version, dataset/version, prompt/version and inference-run identities together with v9.6 research contexts, v9.7 evaluations, v9.8 experiments, evidence, claims, findings, statistical objects, visual objects, studies, publications and artifacts.

## Cross-product exchange

Supported destinations are Platform Core, Workspace, Research Lab, Workbench, Knowledge Library, Decision Studio and Site Intelligence. Exchange packets are transport-neutral and deterministic. Creating a packet does not deliver it. Destination receipt records separately preserve `received`, `accepted`, `imported`, `rejected`, or `failed` states.

## Governance

- Platform Core remains authoritative for governed AI object identity.
- Research Librarian does not register or train models.
- Exchange readiness is structural, not a prediction of destination acceptance.
- Packet creation never implies delivery.
- Destination acceptance requires an explicit receipt.
- No automatic model selection, ranking, truth promotion, or research-validity judgment.
